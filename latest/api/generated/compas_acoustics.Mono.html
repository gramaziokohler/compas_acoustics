Mono

class compas_acoustics.Mono(image_source_order=3, ray_tracing=True, air_absorption=True, n_rays=20000, energy_thres=1e-07, duration=None, receiver_radius=0.5, seed=True, normalize=True, air=None)

Bases: SimulationMethod

Image sources up to image_source_order, and the stochastic ray tracer past it if ray_tracing.

Parameters:
image_source_orderint, default=3

Last specular order; with ray_tracing the ray tracer covers what follows. The search cost grows steeply with the order (image_source_search_seconds()).

ray_tracingbool, default=True

Materials without scattering coefficients scatter 0.05 in every band.

air_absorptionbool, default=True
n_raysint or None, default=20000

Per source; None: a heuristic of at least 50000.

energy_thresfloat, default=1e-7

The product of its reflections in its least absorbed band at which a ray stops; the logged energy is that over the squared distance (Schroeder 2011, eq. 5.54), so the tail has decayed much further.

durationfloat, optional

Of the ray-traced responses, in seconds; default 1.5 times the longest Eyring reverberation time, within 0.5 to 12 s (10 s for a scene that closes no volume), with a warning at the cap. Without ray tracing the responses have their natural length.

receiver_radiusfloat, default=0.5

In metres.

seedbool or int, default=True

True seeds with 42, an int with that value, False or None leaves the run unseeded (generation["seed"] = None).

normalizebool, default=True

Whether the responses are peak-normalized views.

airAirConditions, optional

Its speed of sound and, with air_absorption, its ISO 9613-1 absorption; default AirConditions().

Ray-tracing settings (``n_rays`` to ``seed``) changed with ``ray_tracing=False`` raise ``ValueError``.

Examples

>>> import compas_acoustics as ca
>>> scene = ca.Scene("office")
>>> scene.add_acoustic_objects(ca.AcousticObjectsLibrary.shoebox_room(6, 5, 3, materials=ca.AcousticMaterial("wall", 0.2, 0.1)))
>>> scene.add_source(ca.Source("talker", 1.5, 1.5, 1.6))
>>> scene.add_receiver(ca.Receiver("listener", 4.0, 3.0, 1.2))
>>> responses = scene.simulate(ca.Mono(image_source_order=2, ray_tracing=False))
>>> ir = next(iter(responses.values()))
>>> ir.sampling_rate, ir.n_channels, ir.generation["method"]
(48000, 1, 'mono')

Ray tracing, on by default, adds the late reflections; the seed makes a run repeatable.

>>> responses = scene.simulate(ca.Mono(n_rays=2000, duration=0.5))
>>> ir = next(iter(responses.values()))
>>> ir.ir.shape, ir.generation["seed"]
((24000,), 42)
