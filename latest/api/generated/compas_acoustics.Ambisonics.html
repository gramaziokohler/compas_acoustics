Ambisonics

class compas_acoustics.Ambisonics(reference=None, sh_order=1, normalization='SN3D', *, tail=None, image_source_order=3, n_slopes=1, decay_db=30.0, duration=None, directional_absorption=True, late_envelope=None, diffraction=False, air_absorption=None, seed=True, normalize=True, air=None, scattering=False)

Bases: _HybridMethod

Ambisonic room impulse responses: image sources, optional edge diffraction, a directional FDN.

As Binaural, with every early path encoded at its direction and every delay line projected from its direction at sh_order. Responses are ACN in normalization, stored under 'ambisonics'.

Parameters:
referencestr, dict, Mono or None

As Binaural.

sh_orderint, default=1

The network has 96 delay lines up to third order (Kirsch et al. 2023), above that the smallest equal-area grid supporting the order. The input gain sqrt(num_delays) offsets the output’s 1 / sqrt(num_delays), so the late level does not depend on the count.

normalization{“SN3D”, “N3D”}, default=”SN3D”

ACN channel order, W = 1 for a unit plane wave.

tail{None, “reference”, “ray_tracing”, “eyring”, “sabine”}

Where the decay target comes from: fitted to reference; fitted to a Mono(ray_tracing=True) run of its default settings with the method’s air_absorption; or predicted from volume, area and mean absorption (warns where the room is not diffuse). None: "reference" with a reference, else "ray_tracing".

image_source_orderint, default=3

The last order of the early field; it also drives the network.

n_slopesint, default=1

Decay slopes fitted to the reference; 0 lets DecayFitNet (Goetz et al. 2022) decide (needs multislope). An Eyring or Sabine tail has one.

decay_dbfloat, default=30.0

Single-slope fit range from -5 dB: 30 is T30, 20 is T20.

durationfloat, optional

In seconds; default 1.5 times the longest target RT. A reference the method runs itself has the mono method’s default length whatever the output’s; the directional histogram takes duration. A reference shorter than 2/3 of its reverberation time warns.

directional_absorptionbool, default=True

Weight each delay line with the reflection of the boundary it faces.

late_envelopebool, optional

Shape the late field: attenuated per band to the reference’s late energy, each delay line given its direction’s share of the energy the rays bring (directional histogram, Schroeder 2011). The rays are traced by Mono(ray_tracing=True) at image_source_order, also with a given reference. Default on for a reference or ray-tracing tail; an Eyring or Sabine tail has no reference, so True raises.

diffraction{False, True, “udfa”, “btms”}, default=False

Edge diffraction around obstacles and at room edges that open wider than 180 deg; True is "udfa". "btms" is far slower than "udfa".

air_absorptionbool, optional

On the image sources and diffracted paths. Default the reference’s setting, or True; it must agree with the reference’s, since the early field and the decay target share it.

seedbool or int, default=True

For the feedback matrices and delay jitter: True seeds with 42, an int with that value, False or None leaves them unseeded (generation["seed"] = None). One seed gives both methods the same late field.

normalizebool, default=True

Whether the responses are peak-normalized views.

airAirConditions, optional

Speed of sound and ISO 9613-1 absorption for the image sources, diffracted paths, predicted decay and the reference run. Default the references’ air, else AirConditions(); it must agree with the references’.

scatteringbool, default=False

Surface scattering after Ewert et al. (2025): each image keeps its specular part, prod sqrt(1 - s), in the early field; its scattered part, prod sqrt(s), enters the delay lines of its hemisphere through an allpass cascade. Needs a second image source search.

Attributes:
reference_rirsdict or None

After compute(), the references used: given, read from the graph, or run.

resultsdict

After compute(), the HybridResult per pair.

num_delaysint

96 up to third order, more above (default_num_delays()).

Examples

>>> import compas_acoustics as ca
>>> scene = ca.Scene("office")
>>> scene.add_acoustic_objects(ca.AcousticObjectsLibrary.shoebox_room(6, 5, 3, materials=ca.AcousticMaterial("wall", 0.2, 0.1)))
>>> scene.add_source(ca.Source("talker", 1.5, 1.5, 1.6))
>>> scene.add_receiver(ca.Receiver("listener", 4.0, 3.0, 1.2))
>>> ir = next(iter(scene.simulate(ca.Ambisonics(tail="eyring", sh_order=2)).values()))  
>>> ir.n_channels, ir.ambisonic_order  
(9, 2)
