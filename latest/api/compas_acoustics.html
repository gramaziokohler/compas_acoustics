compas_acoustics

Scene and model

Scene

The acoustic model of a room: its surfaces with materials, sources, receivers, and the impulse responses simulated for them.

AcousticObject

A surface of the scene with its material.

AcousticObjectsLibrary

Factories of commonly used acoustic objects.

RoomGeometry

Every surface of a scene as one room: welded, oriented, materials on SYNTHESIS_BANDS.

Source

A sound source: position, directivity and facing, an optional sound file, and its level.

Receiver

A receiver with an orientation, the frame of its ambisonic responses: x is direction, y its left, z up.

AirConditions

Air at a temperature (deg C), relative humidity (%) and static pressure (Pa), within Cramer's 0 to 30 deg C and 75 to 102 kPa.

Materials

AcousticMaterial

Absorption, scattering and transmission coefficients per octave band of Defaults.DEFAULT_FREQUENCY_BANDS.

MaterialCategory

Broad groups of acoustic materials, as the library is organised.

MaterialsLibrary

The materials of a folder of JSON files, one per material, looked up by name.

Simulation methods

Binaural and Ambisonics need Python 3.11 or newer.

SimulationMethod

A way to compute impulse responses.

Mono

Image sources up to image_source_order, and the stochastic ray tracer past it if ray_tracing.

Binaural

Binaural room impulse responses: image sources, optional edge diffraction, a directional FDN.

Ambisonics

Ambisonic room impulse responses: image sources, optional edge diffraction, a directional FDN.

Results and analysis

ImpulseResponse

An impulse response between a source and a receiver.

Analyser

Room acoustic parameters of an impulse response.

EnergyMap

The energy of a scene's sources at many points, per octave band and time bin, in Pa^2 s / p0^2.

regularized_fitness

Mean absolute error to target plus k times the sample standard deviation; lower is better.

Rays and reflection paths

Ray

A ray traced through a scene, the polyline [source_position, hit_1, hit_2, ...].

ScatteredRay

A scattered ray born at a reflection point of a specular ray.

RayField

Traced rays, with filters and display.

SpecularPath

An image-source path [source_position, hit_1, ..., hit_order, receiver_position].

SpecularReflections

The image-source paths between sources and receivers: a RayField of SpecularPath.

Audio

Auraliser

Convolves impulse responses with their anechoic sounds and sums them.

AudioPlayer

Plays a sound file or an array, on Windows and macOS.
