The Grasshopper components are now a compiled plugin (`CompasAcoustics.gha`) instead of CPython script components: a definition saved
with the old components does not open with the new ones. Delete the old components in it and place the new ones from the toolbar; they have

### Added

* Added `Scene.simulate(method)` as the one way to simulate, with the methods `Mono(image_source_order=3, ray_tracing=True, ...)`, `Binaural(...)` and `Ambisonics(...)` in `compas_acoustics.simulation_methods`.
* Added `Scene.sampling_rate` (default 48000, serialised with the scene), the rate at which every method runs; the Acoustic Scene component has the input.
* Added `Binaural` and `Ambisonics`, binaural and ambisonic room impulse responses from one hybrid engine: image sources, optional edge diffraction (`diffraction="udfa"` or `"btms"`) and a directional feedback delay network tail, ported from hybrid-rir; needs Python >= 3.11. `Binaural(hrtf=None)` takes `"kemar"` (the default) or a SOFA file and records it in `generation["hrtf"]`; `Ambisonics(sh_order=1, normalization="SN3D")` gives ACN channels. Responses are stored under `'binaural'` and `'ambisonics'`.
* Added `tail=...` to `Binaural` and `Ambisonics`, with a reference response, the method's own ray-traced reference, or an Eyring or Sabine prediction as decay target.
* Added edge diffraction at the edges of the room's own boundary (re-entrant corners) to the hybrid engine.
* Added `scattering=False` to `Binaural` and `Ambisonics`, surface scattering after Ewert et al. (2025) as RAZR 0.96.3 implements it: each image's specular part, the product of `sqrt(1 - s)` over its reflections, stays in the early field, and its scattered part, the product of `sqrt(s)`, enters the delay lines on its own hemisphere through an allpass cascade; checked against RAZR in `tests/hybrid/data/razr_scattering`. Off by default.
* Added `compas_acoustics.hybrid.synthesis.prepare`, `compas_acoustics.hybrid.ambisonics.render_ambisonics` (with `ambisonic_output_matrix` and `default_num_delays`, moved from `hybrid.late`) and `compas_acoustics.hybrid.binaural.render_binaural` (`HrtfSet`): one format-neutral core per pair (its early paths and delay lines, `HybridCore`) and two renderers; the binaural one filters every path and every delay line with the HRIR measured nearest its direction. Each renderer evaluates every path's spectrum, and every UDFA transfer function, once, and applies the late envelope (`HybridCore.shaped_lines`) against its own omnidirectional channel.
* Added `HybridResult.late_lines` and `HybridCore.line_directions`: the late field per delay line before it is rendered, and each line's direction in the receiver frame.
* Added `Mono(ray_tracing=True)`, hybrid-rir's ray tracer on top of the image sources (`n_rays`, `energy_thres`, `duration`, `receiver_radius`, `seed`).
* Added `AirConditions(temperature=20, humidity=50, pressure=101325)`: speed of sound after Cramer (1993) and air absorption after ISO 9613-1, for every method, recorded in `ImpulseResponse.generation`.
* Added `RoomGeometry`, the scene's surfaces as one validated room (welded, outward oriented, closedness checked, coplanar faces merged), with `to_pra()` and `is_inside()`.
* Added `AcousticObject(role="boundary" | "obstacle")` and `AcousticObject.from_obj(path, materials=None)`, a list with one object per OBJ group, every one a boundary; groups `materials` does not cover have none until they are assigned, and `AcousticObject.material` accepts only an `AcousticMaterial` or `None`.
* Added `AcousticObject.assign_roles_by_group(objects, roles)` and `assign_materials_by_group(objects, materials)` to mark OBJ groups as obstacles and give them materials after reading, and `AcousticObject.group`, the OBJ group an object was read from (not serialised); a group name that none of the objects has raises a `ValueError`.
* Added `Receiver.up`, `rotation`, `frame` and `Receiver.from_frame()`: the orientation sets the axes of ambisonic responses (x facing, y left, z up).
* Added `compas_acoustics.room.diffuse` (`predicted_rt`, `mean_absorption`, `DiffuseFieldWarning`): Sabine and Eyring reverberation times.
* Added JSON serialisation (`compas.json_dumps`) for `Scene`, `AcousticObject`, `Source`, `Receiver`, `ImpulseResponse`, `Ray`, `ScatteredRay` and `RayField`; the IR graph is not serialised.
* Added `Scene.impulse_responses(attribute)`, `Scene.simulation_methods()` and `attribute=` on the IR graph methods: each method stores its responses under its own name.
* Added `ImpulseResponse` with peak normalization (`denormalized()`, `normalization_factor`, `level_db`), `to_wav()`, `from_wav()`, `generation`, `resampled()`, `ambisonic_order`, `normalization`, `channel_order` and `direct_sound_index()`.
* Added `AcousticAnalyser.stage_support()` (ST_early and ST_late, ISO 3382-1 Annex C) and `sound_strength()` (G, needs pyrato >= 1.0), and `G` in the analysis tables.
* Added `AcousticAnalyser` accepting an `ImpulseResponse`, analysed at its raw level and sampling rate.
* Added `Auraliser.format` (`mono`, `binaural` or `ambisonic`, that of its impulse responses, which must share one), `level` (`relative`, `individual`, `none`), `headroom_db`, and lists of impulse responses and sound files as input.
* Added `RayField`, `Ray` and `ScatteredRay` for traced rays (`RayField.from_scene`), with filtering, truncation and viewer display.
* Added `Source.directivity`, `Source.sound_file` (the `Auraliser` mixes a multichannel file down to mono when it reads it), `Receiver.direction`, `radius` and `received_spls`.
* Added `AcousticMaterial.category` and `data_origin`, `MaterialsLibrary.materials_by_category`, preset materials, and `tools/import_backend_materials.py`.
* Added `AudioPlayer` for `.wav` files and numpy arrays, with `normalize=True`.
* Added `compas_acoustics.regularized_fitness`, exported from the package.
* Added a warning before an image source search that will take long (`simulation_methods.image_source_search_seconds()`).
* Added `compas_acoustics.worker`: runs compas_acoustics in a second Python 3.12, for the Grasshopper plugin and for Rhino 8's Python 3.9: a local HTTP service on a free port with a token per session that takes and returns `compas_pb` payloads (`client()`, `simulate`, `analyse`, and in `worker.api` the functions the components call).
* Added `compas_acoustics.pb`, a compas_pb plugin: the Data types as protobuf messages (`pb/protobuf_defs/.../acoustics.proto`), their Python bindings, converters registered through the `compas_pb.plugins` entry point, `SCHEMA` (which the plugin checks the worker against) and `plain()`; `compas_pb >= 1.2` is a requirement. `invoke generate` regenerates the Python and C# bindings.
* Added `anechoic_soundfiles_library` (bassoon, female and male speech, trumpet, viola) and `hrtfs_library` (KEMAR, free-field compensated, 48 kHz), with READMEs that credit the sources and licences.
* Added `Defaults.HRTF_LIBRARY_PATH`, `ANECHOIC_SOUNDFILES_LIBRARY_PATH` and `PARAM_NAMES`.
* Added `pyFDN` and `multislope` to the requirements on Python >= 3.11, for the hybrid method (pyFDN brings flamo and PyTorch), and a CI job for it: one `pip install compas_acoustics`, no extra.
* Added the Grasshopper plugin `CompasAcoustics.gha` (C#, `csharp/CompasAcoustics.sln`, `invoke build-gha`): typed parameters for the compas_acoustics data, value lists and buttons next to unconnected inputs, and the worker as the only Python it runs, so STI, G and the hybrid method are always available. It needs Rhino 8 and the .NET 8 SDK to build.
* Added the components Simulate Binaural and Simulate Ambisonics (with a `run` button, their last input), Simulate Mono (was Simulate Ray Tracing), Material Library categories, and the inputs `level` (Auralise), `normalize` (Audio Player, Simulate), `method` (Deconstruct IR Graph), `direction` and `plane` (Receiver, which outputs the receiver only and keeps it upright: +z up, no roll).
* Added the input `role` (a value list: `boundary` or `obstacle`) to the Grasshopper Acoustic Object component; a wrong role or material is an error on the component instead of a traceback.
* Added the IR graph of the Grasshopper plugin: Simulate Mono, Simulate Binaural and Simulate Ambisonics output the responses of their method (`pyroomacoustics`, `binaural`, `ambisonics`), and Deconstruct IR Graph reads them, so it has no `method` input.
* Changed the Grasshopper Check Channel component to count the channels of any sound: an audio file path, an impulse response, a Source with a sound file, or an array of samples such as the signals of Auralise (the input is now `sound`).
* Changed the Grasshopper Auralise component to also show the warnings of building the auraliser (impulse responses resampled to a common rate, sounds mixed down to mono), and the Audio Player to remark at which rate it plays an array that comes without one.
* Changed the image source order input of the Grasshopper Simulate Mono (`max_order`), Simulate Binaural and Simulate Ambisonics (`image_source_order`) components to `ism_order`; the Simulate Mono description gave the default as 6, the method's default is 3.
* Changed the order of the inputs of the Grasshopper Auralise component to `irs`, `anechoic_sounds`, `level`, `mix_sources`, `save_file_path`, `save_file_name`, `save`.
* Changed the Grasshopper Auralise component to keep the format of its impulse responses, without the inputs `output_format` and `hrtf`: mono responses (Simulate Mono) give mono signals, binaural ones binaural, ambisonic ones the undecoded ACN/SN3D signal, and the file saved has that many channels. Binaural signals come from Simulate Binaural and its `hrtf`. With `mix_sources`, responses of different formats at one receiver are an error. It convolves in Rhino's Python, without the worker.
* Changed the Grasshopper Audio Player component to warn for a signal of more than two channels (an ambisonic one) instead of an error, and `AudioPlayer.play_array` to play a mono signal on both channels.
* Renamed the input `sound` of the Grasshopper Audio Player component to `audio` and ordered its inputs `audio`, `sampling_rate`, `normalize`, `play`, `stop`.
* Renamed the inputs `signals` and `multiconvolve` of the Grasshopper Auralise component to `anechoic_sounds` and `mix_sources`: `mix_sources` sums the sources at each receiver into one signal.
* Changed the order of the inputs of the Grasshopper Source component to `position`, `name`, `direction`, `directivity`, `sound_file`.
* Changed `AudioPlayer` to read the audio devices again before it plays an array, so that headphones connected after Rhino started are the output.
* Added the input `select` (a value list: `all` or one of EDT, T20, T30, C50, C80, D50, DRR, Ts, STI, G) to the Grasshopper Deconstruct Parameter component: a selected parameter comes out as plain lists, `all` as one branch per parameter. Its `parameters` input takes the list.
* Renamed the Grasshopper Simulate Pyroomacoustics component to Simulate Mono, after the method it runs, `Mono` (pyroomacoustics; graph attribute and worker method `'mono'`, `'data'` as before) (the same GUID: saved definitions load it) and its output `ir_graph` to `mono_ir_graph`, after the format of its responses, as Simulate Binaural's `binaural_ir_graph` and Simulate Ambisonics' `ambisonic_ir_graph`.
* Added the input `source_idx` to the Grasshopper Deconstruct IR Graph component (inputs `ir_graph`, `source_idx`, `receiver_idx`): with `receiver_idx` it picks source-receiver combinations, e.g. a single impulse response for Analyse Impulse Response. Sources are indexed in the order of the scene's sources; an index out of range is a warning.
* Added `compas_acoustics.analyser.time_response` (level in dB re the peak of all channels, or amplitude) and `frequency_response` (fractional-octave smoothed, in dB re the peak), per channel; levels are floored 100 dB below the peak.
* Added the outputs `time` and `frequency` and the input `time_scale` (a value list: `db` or `linear`) to the Grasshopper Analyse Impulse Response component: the impulse response and its 1/3-octave smoothed frequency response from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, of the W channel of an ambisonic response and both ears of a binaural one. Plot 2D draws them as a curve per channel, on a time axis or a log frequency axis with ticks at 50, 100, 200, 500 ... 20000 Hz, reduced to the minimum and maximum of 1000 bins; a level in dB runs from its floor on the x axis to its peak, an amplitude is symmetric about zero with the x axis at zero. The time output in `linear` ends 5 % after its Schroeder curve falls 60 dB, in `db` it is the whole response, (`time_response(..., trim_db=...)`); the parameters and the frequency output use the whole response. `AcousticParameter.from_time_response` and `from_frequency_response` replace `from_ir`, which Plot 2D could not draw.

* Changed the Grasshopper Plot 2D chart to leave half a band's spacing between the y axis and the first frequency band.

* Added a status to the Grasshopper Simulate Binaural and Simulate Ambisonics components for when they are not running: it says `done` with the tail (and the ambisonic order) of the last result on the graph, `settings changed, press Run` when its inputs differ from that result, and `press Run` only when there is none.

* Added `examples/office_hybrid.py`, which runs both methods on an office.
* Added a getting-started section to the README, and `Examples` sections that `invoke testdocs` can run in the docstrings of `Scene`, `Mono`, `Binaural`, `Ambisonics`, `AcousticAnalyser` and `Auraliser`.
* Added `SpecularReflections.from_scene(scene, max_order=3)`, the image-source paths from each source to each receiver for inspection, with `filter(source, ray_index, receiver_index, order)`. It is a `RayField` of `SpecularPath` rays, so it plays in a billiard (`particles`) and displays like traced rays.
* Added source directivity to `RayField.from_scene`, and so to Visualise Rays and Visualise Billiard: each source emits its rays with the density of its radiated energy, (p + (1 - p) cos theta)^2 about its `direction` (a deterministic lattice; a cardioid sends nothing straight back, a figure-of-eight nothing sideways); within a spotlight cone the cone's lattice is thinned to that density. An omnidirectional source emits the same rays as before.
* Added `RayField.particles(time_ms)` and `RayField.duration_ms`, the rays' positions at a time, for a billiard view.
* Added RAVEN's directional ray-tracing histogram to the hybrid's late envelope (`late_envelope=True` of `Binaural` and `Ambisonics`, the default with a reference or ray-tracing tail): pyroomacoustics keeps one histogram per receiver direction (`raytracing.directional_histograms`, one bin per delay line, binned by the direction a ray arrives from; Lentz, Schroeder and Vorlaender 2007; Schroeder 2011, Sec. 5.3.4), and after the level per band follows the reference as before, each delay line is scaled by the square root of its direction's share of the rays' energy over its share of the lines' energy (`hybrid.envelope.directional_shares`, `shape_late_directional`), both pooled over neighbouring lines with the weights of Kirsch et al. (2023, Eq. 4), so the total is kept and a direction facing an absorbing wall decays as the rays say it does; held once the rays have decayed 60 dB, RAVEN's particle cutoff. The histogram is traced by the method itself, also with a given reference: one more ray-tracing run per scene. Against exact image-source shoeboxes with uneven absorption the per-direction late level error fell from 1.7-12.9 dB to 0.9-3.6 dB and the per-direction T30 error from 19-127 % to 5-13 %; in the AVARIG offices the per-beam late level correlation with Odeon rose from 0.59 to 0.83 (order 3, 1 kHz); the omnidirectional level and decay are unchanged.
* Added the Grasshopper Visualise Reflections component: image-source paths of a source-receiver pair by order, output as `reflections`, which Visualise Billiard plays.
* Added the Grasshopper Visualise Rays component: rays traced from one source (`source_idx`) or all, for display and Visualise Billiard.

### Changed

* Changed the geometric method of the lab to report progress and to stop at the next source when cancelled.
* Changed the worker to install `compas_acoustics[lab]` (trimesh, embreex, mesh2scattering): reinstall the worker to use the lab. NumCalc, which `bem` needs, is a separate setup step.
* Changed the default `method` of `measure_scattering` to `"geometric"` (it was `"kirchhoff"`); pass `method="kirchhoff"` for the previous behaviour, which needs neither `trimesh` nor Python >= 3.11. Every method now defaults to the `"standard"` source preset.
* Changed the worker's playback API: `play(sound, normalize=True, gain_db=0.0, start=0.0)` returns the duration; `pause`, `resume`, `stop`, `playback_status` and `sound_samples` are new.
* Changed how the hybrid's early renderer and late envelope compute, not what: `early.render_images` and `render_transfers` accumulate the paths' spectra as matrix products over chunks of paths instead of one outer product per path, and `envelope.octave_band_split` applies pyfar's reconstructing bank as one FFT per channel and one inverse FFT per band (zero-padded past the filter length, the linear convolution pyfar computes) with the bank designed once per sampling rate. Responses agree with the previous code to rounding (5e-14 of their peak) over eight scenes (shoeboxes at orders 1 to 3, binaural and ambisonic, with scattering, an Eyring tail, 32 kHz, the two Grasshopper rooms, a furnished office with diffraction).
* Changed `AcousticAnalyser.speech_transmission_index` to reduce the modulation transfer function it already has with pyrato's `_sti_calc` (IEC 60268-16:2020 Annex A.2.1) instead of calling `speech_transmission_index_indirect`, which computes the same MTF again: the same STI, exactly, in half the time; without `_sti_calc` the indirect method is called as before.
* Changed the ray tracer's defaults to what the run needs: `energy_thres` 1e-7 (pyroomacoustics' own; was hybrid-rir's 1e-8), since `libroom` stops a ray on the product of its wall reflections while the receiver logs that energy over the squared distance, so between 1e-8 and 1e-4 no parameter moved beyond the seed spread in four rooms and 1e-8 cost 25 % more in a reverberant one; and `duration` 1.5 times the longest Eyring reverberation time within 0.5 to 12 s (was 2.5 times; ODEON asks for at least 2/3 of the longest RT and RAZR takes 1.0, and 1.5 keeps T30 where Eyring is 18 to 31 % short, measured down to 0.67). The runs `Hybrid` makes itself, a ray-traced reference and the directional histogram, take the same default (was 2.5 times): over five shoeboxes its response is identical at 1.5, 2.0 and 2.5 times, and 1.0 moves T30 at 125 Hz by 9 to 20 %. A reference given to `Hybrid` that is shorter than 2/3 of its reverberation time warns (ODEON's rule; an ideal decay cut there reads T30 2.4 % short), since pyFDN's decay fit silently shrinks its range to what the response has (measured: a 1.0-times reference read T30 at 125 Hz 25 % short in a room with curtain walls, 1.5 times gave the 2.5-times result). A ray-traced response that ends before 2/3 of its own longest T30 warns with the length to rerun with (`Pyroomacoustics._warn_if_cut_short`): a furnished office with an absorbing ceiling over hard walls decays in 2 s where Eyring predicts 0.64 s, so no multiple of the prediction is safe there. A room whose Eyring prediction hits the 12 s cap also warns (`raytracing.default_duration`): its rays live for hundreds of reflections, usually from a material with no low-frequency absorption on a large surface.
* Changed the JSON data type of every `compas_acoustics` class to `compas_acoustics/<ClassName>` (was `compas_acoustics.<module>/<ClassName>`), so that saved data no longer depends on the module a class lives in; the materials library is stored that way. Files saved before do not load.
* Changed the modules: `ray` and `specular` are one module, `rays`; `acoustic_material` and `materials_library` are `materials`; `geometry` is `acoustic_object`; `numerical.fitness` is `fitness`; `room_geometry`, `air`, `diffuse`, `pra_converter`, `raytracing` and `_pyfar_constants` are the subpackage `room` (`room.geometry`, `room.air`, `room.diffuse`, `room.pra`, `room.raytracing`, `room._pyfar_constants`), which exports `RoomGeometry` and `AirConditions`; `room.raytracing` holds both pyroomacoustics runs (`image_source_rirs`, `ray_traced_rirs`) and the directional histograms, the backend of `Mono` and of the binaural and ambisonics methods; `binaural` is `hybrid.binaural`. The classes are exported from `compas_acoustics` as before.
* Changed `Defaults().DEFAULT_SPEED_OF_SOUND` to the speed of sound of `AirConditions()` (343.98 m/s at 20 C and 50 %, was 343), the air every simulation runs in without one given. `Ray`, `ScatteredRay` and `RayField` no longer take or store a `sound_speed` (it was 343 on its own and was serialised with every ray): their travel times are at this default; ray fields saved with it still load.
* Changed the hybrid engine to use 96 delay lines up to third ambisonic order, and always for `Binaural` (`hybrid.late.MIN_DELAYS`; 16 before at orders 0 and 1, 36 at 2, 64 at 3): the count Kirsch, Wendt and van de Par (2023) run their network with whatever the number of rendered directions, so that the tail's character does not change with the count, and the one they name for offline simulation; RAZR 0.96.3 allows up to 96 and defaults to 24 for real time. In the binaural response each line is heard through its own HRIR. A 1.5 s response takes about 1 s longer per source-receiver pair. The methods take no `num_delays` or `drive_gain`: the count follows from `sh_order` (`Ambisonics`) or is 96 (`Binaural`), and the input gain `sqrt(num_delays)` offsets the output's `1 / sqrt(num_delays)`, so the late level does not depend on the count; `hybrid.synthesis.prepare` keeps both. The delay-line grid depends on the count alone (`fdn_directions(num_delays)`); whether it carries an ambisonic order is `ambisonic_output_matrix`'s check.
* Changed how the last rendered image-source order drives the hybrid's network: each image now enters the delay lines of the hemisphere opposite its arrival, weighted by the cosine to each line (`hybrid.scattering.scattering_weights(..., opposite=True)`), the mapping of Ewert et al. (2025, Sec. 3.1.2) as RAZR 0.96.3's `fdn_smart_ch_mapping` implements it, checked against RAZR's FDN input in `tests/hybrid/data/razr_scattering`. Before, each image entered the one line nearest the opposite direction, hybrid-rir's own choice without a reference. On four shoeboxes against an exact image-source room the late level now lies within 0.5 dB (before -1.5 to +1.8 dB); `hybrid.early.route_to_directions` stays as the one-hot form the tests compare against.
* Changed the spelling of identifiers to `normalization` and `normalized` everywhere, including `ImpulseResponse.normalization` (the ambisonic convention) and its JSON metadata key.
* Changed `AudioPlayer` to raise errors and `warnings.warn` instead of printing them; the Audio Player component shows them as messages. An unsupported sound type raises a `TypeError`.
* Changed `seed` of `Mono`, `Binaural` and `Ambisonics` (was 0) to `True` by default, which seeds with 42 (`DEFAULT_SEED`); an int seeds with that value; `False` (or `None`) leaves the run unseeded, a new draw each run, recorded as `generation["seed"] = None`. Anything else raises a `ValueError`. `RayField.from_scene(random_seed=)` follows the same rule.
* Changed the hybrid engine to drive the network with the last rendered image source order, as Wendt et al. (2014), RAZR and Ewert et al. (2025) do; hybrid-rir used the next, unrendered order. The search stops at `image_source_order` (29 minutes became 14 seconds on BRAS CR2).
* Changed `Binaural` and `Ambisonics` without a reference to run `Mono(ray_tracing=True, image_source_order=3, n_rays=100000, seed=42)` and fit the decay to it.
* Changed the hybrid's 35 Hz high-pass to a causal filter, which removes pre-arrival ringing in the diffracted paths.
* Changed every simulation to run in `AirConditions()` (20 C, 50 %, 343.98 m/s) unless `air=` is given; before, the methods used different air tables.
* Changed simulated impulse responses to 48000 Hz (were always 16000 Hz) and to the package scale (free-field direct sound `1 / (4 pi r)`).
* Changed `AcousticObject.__init__` to take one `material`; `AcousticObjectsLibrary.shoebox_room()` returns a list of six single-material objects and takes `materials`.

* Changed the `AcousticAnalyser` methods to name the quantity: `reverberation_times`, `clarity_definition`, `direct_to_reverberant_ratio`, `speech_transmission_index`, `stage_support`, `sound_strength`.

* Fixed a simulation failing on a Source whose `sound_file` is not a RIFF WAV (an AIFF or FLAC file; the library's `basoon.wav` is an AIFF): the file is read with soundfile.
* Fixed the hybrid engine counting an image source twice when its path passes through an edge (+6 dB on that path); `hybrid.early.image_sources` now keeps one image per position, as `ShoeBox` does.
* Fixed low-frequency bias in EDT, C50, C80, D50, DRR and Ts caused by the delay of causal octave filters.
* Fixed `speech_transmission_index` returning NaN for impulse responses shorter than 1.6 s.
* Fixed wheels that were missing the materials library, the HRTFs, the sound files and the Grasshopper components.
* Fixed `Auraliser` modifying the caller's dicts, ignoring 1D responses and multichannel sounds, and wrapping energy around when resampling.
* Fixed simulations above 16 kHz with air absorption, which failed on a 7 against 8 octave band mismatch.
* Fixed `Receiver` and `Source` losing name, orientation, directivity and settings in JSON and `copy()`.
* Fixed NaN responses for a source at a receiver's position (now a `ValueError`), and unrelated errors for points inside a closed obstacle, scenes of obstacles only, or too few walls.
* Fixed the hybrid failing on a fully absorbing surface, and counting a wall hit on a diffraction apex as a reflection.
* Fixed `display_in_viewer` guards that treated `compas.is_rhino()` as a boolean, and the default HRTF path that pointed at a missing file.
* Fixed the Grasshopper Auralise and Analyse IR components using a fixed sample rate.
* Fixed `AcousticObject.from_obj` reading OBJ files in the platform's encoding: a UTF-8 byte order mark dropped the first vertex, and non-ASCII group names were garbled on Windows; files are read as UTF-8.
* Fixed obstacle faces lying on a boundary face being counted when the scene is not aligned with the axes: the test used the distance along each axis instead of the distance to the face's plane.

* Removed the two MIT KEMAR HRTF sets and `speech.wav`.
