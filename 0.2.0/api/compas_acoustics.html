    Analyser ¶

    Analyser(ir)

    Room acoustic parameters of an impulse response (analyse).

    Every window starts at onset, the direct sound of channel 0 as pyfar.dsp.find_impulse_response_start finds it with the 20 dB criterion of ISO 3382-1:2009, p. 22.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    ir (list, ndarray or ImpulseResponse)

    (samples,) or (samples, channels). LF and LFC take the first-order channels of an ambisonic ImpulseResponse, every other parameter channel 0 (the omnidirectional component, or the left ear). An ImpulseResponse is analysed at its raw level and sets sampling_rate.

    		 required

    Examples:

    >>> import compas_acoustics as ca
>>> scene = ca.Scene("office")
>>> room = ca.AcousticObjectsLibrary.shoebox_room(6, 5, 3, materials=ca.AcousticMaterial("wall", 0.2, 0.1))
>>> scene.add_acoustic_objects(room)
>>> scene.add_source(ca.Source("talker", 1.5, 1.5, 1.6))
>>> scene.add_receiver(ca.Receiver("listener", 4.0, 3.0, 1.2))
>>> responses = scene.simulate(ca.Mono(ray_tracing=True, n_rays=2000, duration=0.5))
>>> analyser = ca.Analyser(next(iter(responses.values())))
>>> results = analyser.analyse()
>>> results["freqs"]
[125, 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000]
>>> results["T30_overall"] > 0
True

    Methods:¶

    analyse ¶
    analyse() -> dict

    Every parameter of _PARAM_NAMES per band and as '<name>_overall'.

    The overall values of EDT, T20, T30, C50, C80, D50 and Ts average 500 and 1000 Hz, after the single-number averages of ISO 3382-1:2009 Table A.1; the same average for all seven is the package's choice. The others have their own. The per-band 'STI' values are the modulation transfer indices, 'STI_overall' the STI.

    clarity_definition() -> dict

    C50, C80 and D50 per band (ISO 3382-1:2009).

    Early and late parts are cut from the broadband response before causal band filtering, so filter delay and ringing stay with the part they belong to, the package's choice.

    direct_to_reverberant_ratio ¶
    direct_to_reverberant_ratio() -> dict

    Direct-to-reverberant ratio per band and for 700-4000 Hz, in dB.

    The energy from 1 ms before to 5 ms after the direct sound over the energy after it. The window and the 700-4000 Hz band of 'DRR_overall' (a fourth-order Butterworth band-pass, butter(2, ..., "bandpass")) are the package's choice. Per band the two parts are cut before filtering; for 'DRR_overall' the response is filtered first.

    lateral_fraction ¶
    lateral_fraction() -> dict

    Early lateral energy fractions J_LF and J_LFC per band (ISO 3382-1:2009 Annex A).

    J_LF is the figure-of-eight energy 5-80 ms after the direct sound over the omnidirectional energy 0-80 ms; J_LFC takes |p_L p| for p_L^2 (Kleiner 1989; Bradley 1994). The figure-of-eight is the first-order dipole of the response, horizontal in the receiver's frame and perpendicular to the source direction (the receiver's y axis for a source straight above or below). Windows are cut before band filtering, the package's choice.

    Returns:

    Type Description
    dict

    'freqs', 'LF' and 'LFC' per band, and 'LF_overall' and 'LFC_overall', the mean of 125-1000 Hz. NaN for a response that is not an ambisonic ImpulseResponse (ACN, SN3D or N3D).

    reverberation_times ¶
    reverberation_times() -> dict

    EDT, T20 and T30 per band (ISO 3382-1:2009).

    Schroeder (1965, J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 37(3)) backward integration of the zero-phase band (_band_from_onset) from the direct sound to the last non-zero sample, normalized to 0 dB at its start, then linear regression. No noise compensation: truncate a measured response at its noise floor first.

    Auraliser ¶

    Auraliser(impulse_responses, anechoic_sounds=None, sampling_rate: int = None, level: str = 'relative', headroom_db: float = -1.0)

    Convolves impulse responses with their anechoic sounds and sums them.

    The signal has the format of its responses (format): 'mono', 'binaural' or 'ambisonic' (ACN/SN3D at the highest order present; N3D is converted). Formats cannot be mixed: there is no decoding. Responses are used at their raw level; level sets the gain on export: 'relative' (one gain for a group of renders, the loudest peaking at headroom_db), 'individual' (each peaks at it) or 'none' (no gain; warns above 0 dBFS).

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    impulse_responses dict or list

    ImpulseResponse objects (ACN/SN3D when they state no convention), or a dict of ids to dicts with 'ir' ((samples,) or (samples, channels)), 'sampling_rate', 'ambisonic_order', 'channel_order' ('acn') and 'normalization' ('sn3d' or 'n3d').

    		 required
    anechoic_sounds dict or list

    File paths, or a dict of ids to dicts with 'filepath': paired by id when both are dicts with the same ids, else by position. Default: the sound_file of each response's source.

    		 None
    sampling_rate int

    Responses and sounds are resampled to it; default that of the first response.

    		 None
    level str

    Default 'relative'.

    		 'relative'
    headroom_db float

    In dBFS; default -1.

    		 -1.0

    Examples:

    >>> import compas_acoustics as ca
>>> sound = ca.Defaults().ANECHOIC_SOUNDFILES_LIBRARY_PATH + "/female_speech.wav"
>>> scene = ca.Scene("office")
>>> room = ca.AcousticObjectsLibrary.shoebox_room(6, 5, 3, materials=ca.AcousticMaterial("wall", 0.2, 0.1))
>>> scene.add_acoustic_objects(room)
>>> scene.add_source(ca.Source("talker", 1.5, 1.5, 1.6, sound_file=sound))
>>> scene.add_receiver(ca.Receiver("listener", 4.0, 3.0, 1.2))
>>> responses = scene.simulate(ca.Mono(ray_tracing=True, n_rays=2000, duration=0.5))
>>> auraliser = ca.Auraliser(list(responses.values()))
>>> auraliser.format, auraliser.convolve().shape[1]
('mono', 1)

    Source ¶

    Source(name: str, x: float, y: float, z: float, directivity: str = None, direction: Vector = None, sound_file: str = None)

    Bases: _PackageDtype, Point

    A sound source: position, directivity and facing, an optional sound file, and its level.

    directivity is a first-order pattern p + (1 - p) cos(theta) about direction, one of 'omnidirectional' (the default), 'cardioid', 'supercardioid', 'hypercardioid' and 'bidirectional' (compas_acoustics.room.pra.CARDIOID_P); the name is checked when a method simulates the source.

    spl is the free-field level on its axis at 1 m per octave band, 125 Hz to 8 kHz, in dB re 20 uPa, 120 dB in every band unless set (the package's choice). On the package scale the direct sound is 1 / (4 pi r) with gain 1 on axis, so Lp = spl + G - 20 dB (Analyser.sound_pressure_level).
